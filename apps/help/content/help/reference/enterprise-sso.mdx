ClassroomHub supports Single Sign-On (SSO) via OpenID Connect (OIDC), allowing your organization members to sign in using your existing identity provider (IdP). For step-by-step setup, start with [Set Up Enterprise SSO](/organization-and-team/enterprise-sso-setup).

:::info[Enterprise Feature]
SSO requires an **Enterprise** plan.

[Contact us](mailto:support@classroom.io.vn) to upgrade.
:::

## Supported Providers

We support OIDC-based SSO with the following identity providers:

- **Okta**
- **Google Workspace**
- **Auth0**

## How It Works

### Academy Login

When users visit your organization's custom domain (e.g., `yourorg.classroom.io.vn`), SSO is automatically detected and they can sign in directly with your IdP.

### Shared App Login

On the main ClassroomHub app (`hub.classroom.io.vn`), users enter their email address first. If their email domain matches your configured SSO domain, they'll be prompted to sign in via SSO.

## Access Policies

### Force SSO

When enabled, all users must sign in via SSO. Email/password login is disabled for your organization. This is recommended for security compliance.

### Auto-Join

When enabled, users who sign in via SSO with a matching email domain are automatically added to your organization. Otherwise, they must be invited first.

:::note[Users can't auto-join]
If a user isn't joining automatically, check that the email domain matches exactly (`company.com`, not `@company.com`), that Auto-Join is enabled in your SSO policy, and that the user has a verified email from your domain.
:::

### Break-Glass Access

Allows organization admins to bypass SSO with email/password in case of emergency (e.g., IdP outage). Keep this enabled for safety.

## User Provisioning

### JIT (Just-In-Time) Provisioning

Users are automatically provisioned when they first sign in via SSO:

1. If the user has a pending invitation, they join with the invited role
2. If auto-join is enabled, they join with the default role (typically Student)
3. If neither, they can join later via invitation

### Role Mapping

Map IdP groups to ClassroomHub roles:

- Admins → `ROLE.ADMIN` (1)
- Tutors → `ROLE.TUTOR` (2)
- Students → `ROLE.STUDENT` (3)

Configure this in your SSO policy settings.

## Security Notes

- SSO secrets (client credentials) are encrypted at rest
- All SSO login attempts are logged for audit purposes
- Force SSO is enforced server-side, not just UI-level
- Session management follows your IdP's policies

## SAML Support

SAML 2.0 support is planned for a future release. Currently, only OIDC is supported.
