---
title: Set Up a Custom Domain
description: Connect and verify a custom domain for your ClassroomHub academy and learn which links use your org URL.
---

Every organization gets a default site at `https://<siteName>.classroom.io.vn`. You can connect your own domain (for example `https://learn.yourcompany.com`) so students see your brand in the browser bar and in links.

## How ClassroomHub picks a base URL

When ClassroomHub builds a link for your organization, it resolves the host in this order:

1. **Verified custom domain** → `https://<your-domain>`
2. **Academy URL** → `https://<siteName>.classroom.io.vn`
3. **Fallback** → `https://hub.classroom.io.vn`

Most customer-facing course links use your org context and stop at step 1 or 2. The `hub.classroom.io.vn` fallback applies when no academy URL is available.

## Set up your domain

<Steps>
<Step>
### Open domain settings

From the admin dashboard, go to **Settings → Organization**, then click **Edit domain** in the Custom Domain section.

</Step>

<Step>
### Enter your domain

Add the hostname you want (for example `learn.yourcompany.com`). Follow the DNS instructions ClassroomHub shows to verify ownership.

</Step>

<Step>
### Wait for verification

Verification can take time while DNS propagates. The domain must show as verified before links use it.

</Step>

<Step>
### Test on the live site

Open your custom domain in a browser. Sign in, open a course landing page, and confirm URLs stay on your domain.

</Step>
</Steps>

:::warning[Verification required]
An unverified custom domain is not used for generated links. Until verification succeeds, ClassroomHub keeps using `<siteName>.classroom.io.vn`.
:::

## Which links use your org URL

| Link type                                | Uses org domain when verified?                 |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Course enrollment (`/course/.../enroll`) | Yes                                            |
| Course invite emails from **People**     | Yes                                            |
| Org site landing page and course catalog | Yes                                            |
| Organization team invite (some flows)    | May still use platform URL until fully aligned |

Course invites are documented in [How to Invite Students](/enrollment-and-students/invite-students). Those links should point at your academy, not `hub.classroom.io.vn`, when your domain is verified.

## Email sender address

Invite and notification emails may still send from the ClassroomHub platform mail address. Your **organization name** appears in the message body; the sender inbox is not fully white-labeled today.

## Student login and redirects

Students should sign in on your academy so sessions and redirects stay on your academy URL. If login sends users to `hub.classroom.io.vn` unexpectedly, check that the custom domain is verified and that you are sharing academy links (not the generic app URL).
