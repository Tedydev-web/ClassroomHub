# Exercise Workspace

## Desktop authoring

Passage and questions remain visible together while the teacher edits the
assessment item. Supporting content occupies the left canvas; response
interactions are authored and ordered on the right.

## Mobile authoring

Passage, Questions, and Preview become explicit workspace modes on small
screens. Each mode preserves context without turning the editor into one
continuous form.
